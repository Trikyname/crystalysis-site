The crystal sits in the middle. The waves come for it.

Every level you pick up upgrades. Stack them into a build, mix weapons and passives. Resist, hold, burst the waves.

Six artifacts. Six maps. Four ways to aim.

  • Each artifact changes how you control the ball, not how big your numbers are: the Ball, the Cannonball, the Katana, the Saw, and two different ways to play the Tether.
  • Weapons, passives and Aspects. Max the right weapon with the right passive and they evolve into a new, better weapon.
  • Fire, ice, kinetic, orbitals, summons and salvo feed into each other, so a passive taken for one reason often improves something you picked for another.
  • Endless runs with no finish line, a Timed Run that ends in a boss fight, and a daily run on a seed everybody shares.
  • Aim with a quick flick or a full slingshot pull.
  • Mouse, controller or touch. Fully offline, in thirteen languages, with online leaderboards when you want them.
